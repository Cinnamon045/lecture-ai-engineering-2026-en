In the context of AI engineering, you can think of a vector database as a specialized storage system designed to handle data in the form of mathematical vectors—which are essentially lists of numbers representing the semantic meaning of data (like text, images, or audio).

Instead of searching for exact keyword matches like a traditional database, these systems allow you to perform "similarity searches" to find items that are contextually or semantically related.

To get a better idea of how these are applied in modern workflows (such as retrieval-augmented generation), I recommend checking the summary for the session where we discuss knowledge retrieval and vector search.
