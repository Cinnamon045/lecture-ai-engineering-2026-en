In the context of the course, `requires_approval=True` is a mechanism used for **Human-in-the-loop** processes.

When you define a tool with this parameter, it acts as a safety measure. Instead of the agent executing the tool immediately, the system pauses to allow a human to review and confirm the action. This is particularly important for operations that are "difficult to undo," such as sending emails, deleting data, or making external API calls.

To understand this better, consider:
*   Why might an autonomous agent need a human to verify its decisions before it performs a task?
*   Think about the workflow of an agent: if it decides to perform an action, where in that loop would a human need to step in to say "yes" or "no"?

You can find more on how this fits into the broader design of agent workflows in the relevant session.
